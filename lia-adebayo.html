[ The studio ]

We make spaces that are generous in their restraint.

Maison Adebayo is an architecture and interior design practice founded by Lia Adebayo. The studio creates contemporary buildings with a deep sensitivity to climate, craft and the rituals of everyday life.

Based in Lagos and working across Africa and Europe, our work moves from the first sketch to the last carefully chosen handle.
